# Admin operations, launch pricing and onboarding — 18 November 2026

## Audit findings and implemented changes

The previous dashboard supported platform totals, the latest 200 businesses, subscription pause/resume/manual grants and administrator access changes. It did not provide detailed business inspection, public-site traffic reporting, editable plan pricing/trial policies or provider diagnostics. Sale-history exports always included all records, and the business brief did not explicitly present its financial figures.

This release adds:

- **Read-only business inspection under the administrator's own identity.** Platform owners/admins can search businesses by name or owner email, open a workspace with a recorded reason, inspect stock, sales, customer balances, team and insights, and preview receipts. Each opening is recorded with actor/time/reason and expires after 15 minutes. No owner authentication token is generated, membership is not changed, and customer mutation RPCs still reject an unrelated platform administrator. Viewer accounts cannot inspect business details. The inspection log is available to platform owners/admins. No account-owner email is sent by this feature.
- **Admin pricing and trial policy controls.** Owners/admins can change a plan's price and availability and change the trial duration/availability for future owners. Each edit requires a reason, checks a revision to prevent overwriting another edit, and records before/after values. Existing purchases, pending checkout amounts and current trial expiries are not rewritten. Team/scan/template limits remain the existing Starter/Growth limits; this screen edits commercial pricing and onboarding policy.
- **Automatic new-owner Growth trials.** A newly created business receives 45 Growth days, including its full existing feature/seat/scan limits. The trial starts when the first business is set up, requires no upfront payment and does not auto-charge. A durable owner claim prevents restart through routine onboarding or staff invitations. Existing businesses are recorded as already claimed and receive no retroactive trial. Existing manual trial controls remain available. When the trial expires, normal paid-access checks apply; old records remain readable/exportable.
- **Public-site traffic reporting.** Admins can review public page views, estimated browser sessions, visited pages, referrer hostnames, daily counts and new-business counts over 7/30/90 days. This is first-party tracking, not a connection to Google Search Console or verified unique-person counting. Private workspace/admin routes and known bots are excluded; Do Not Track is honoured. Browser sessions expire after 30 minutes of inactivity. No raw IP is stored: the write-rate key is a daily salted HMAC. Raw referrer URLs/queries are not stored. The reporting window is 90 days and old events are removed when new visits arrive. Tracking has no historical backfill, and browsers can block it.
- **AI request repairs and diagnostics.** Scanning and business briefs now share a validated JSON client, filter reasoning-only response parts, classify safe provider errors, and fall back to the supported default model on a model-not-found response. The default is `gemini-3.1-flash-lite`; an explicit `GEMINI_MODEL` remains respected. Admin health checks show configured/missing values without revealing keys and can make a small actual AI test request, limited to one test per admin per 30 seconds. Failed daily briefs release their reservation for retry after about a minute rather than trapping the user in the old 15-minute cooldown. Brief snapshots use current transaction/discount data.
- **Explicit money figures in business briefs.** Sales made, estimated gross profit, collections, outstanding customer balances and discounts are calculated by Countiva from its records and displayed alongside the explanation. Available cash is marked **Not tracked**, because operating expenses and withdrawals are not fully recorded; the AI is instructed not to invent a spendable bank/cash balance. Gross profit excludes operating expenses and tax. The sparkle icons on Business insights and the landing-page report preview have been replaced with business chart icons.
- **Dated sale exports.** Sales history can export a chosen day, Monday–Sunday week, calendar month or inclusive custom range. Import & export applies the same selection to CSV and Excel. Dates use Africa/Lagos. Summary exports label initial payments, paid-to-date and balance-to-date clearly; a sales-date selection does not imply that every later repayment occurred within that selected period. The selection shows the number of matching sales.

| Growth, 12 months         | NGN 420,000 |         NGN 35,000 |                           30% |

These are the user-approved initial prices. Runtime prices come from the database catalog; the payment API verifies that the displayed quoted amount still matches before creating a checkout. An old pending checkout retains its original amount and must expire/be completed before a differently priced new checkout for that business.

The bundle ladder protects more revenue than the earlier proposed NGN 120k/210k/360k offers, while rewarding longer commitments. It is not a profitability guarantee. Measure hosting/storage, AI use, payment fees, support, acquisition, abuse and trial conversion. For example, a **target** 70% gross margin on the NGN 35k/month annual equivalent leaves NGN 10.5k/month for direct costs including allocated payment fees; trial acquisition costs and fixed business overhead still need funding. This is a planning ceiling, not an estimate of actual Countiva costs. Keep the existing usage limits and review measured conversion before extending the 45-day trial further. One trial per account does not by itself prevent abuse through multiple fake accounts.

## Connecting the Bachs account next

The existing server integration already creates NGN checkout sessions and verifies signed events and payment amounts before activation. The user confirmed “bots” meant the Bachs merchant account.

Configure the merchant's `BACHS_API_KEY`, `BACHS_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, and preferably `BACHS_ACCOUNT_ID` in Vercel's production environment, then redeploy. Register `https://www.countiva.shop/api/billing/webhook` in Bachs for the integration's supported checkout/payment/refund events (`checkout.completed`, `collection.succeeded`, `refund.paid`). The webhook requires a signed POST; opening it as a normal web page is not a connection test. Never paste API keys or signing secrets into chat, public code or browser settings.

Check **Service health** for configuration presence, then use Bachs's appropriate test/sandbox process to validate a full checkout and signed webhook before accepting real customer payments. Existing tests validate signatures, amounts, currencies and replay behaviour; no merchant connection or real payment was performed during this implementation.
