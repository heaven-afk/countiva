# Report reliability and confirmed business deletion

## Findings and repairs

The report endpoint already returned calculated financials when the AI provider was unavailable. The page rendered successful reports below the saved-report list without bringing them into view. Loading and failure messages also appeared below the content. This explains how a request could dim the button and appear to do nothing, even when it completed or returned an error. Successful reports now receive focus and scroll into view; generation feedback appears beside the button, and failures receive focus. HTML gateway errors, network failures and timeouts have readable messages. A missing database update or server permission issue directs the user to administrator checks instead of implying that their paid plan is insufficient.

The daily AI route previously returned cached financial figures before reading updated online records. It now refreshes those figures while preserving the narrative's preparation time. The page distinguishes device totals, online saved reports, daily narratives and financial timestamps. Unsynced changes must be saved before requesting a new online report. Saved financial snapshots remain fixed at their stated preparation time.

Migration 020 makes brief/report explanation caches aware of the effective analysis level. A same-day upgrade to Business can obtain the deeper explanation without changing the saved financial snapshot. Failed AI explanations can retry after a five-minute cooldown. Calculated reports remain readable when AI is unavailable. AI model fallbacks share one 35-second request budget so database saving can finish within the route's time budget. Missing entitlement connections display a retry control instead of an indefinite skeleton. Changing periods clears the previously displayed report, and exports apply the current plan's projection.

The plan rules remain: Starter has no business insights; Growth offers standard summaries and 1/7/30-day reports; Business adds product margins, stock cover, payment mix, balance aging, prioritised actions and 90-day reports. Previously granted Growth contracts may retain detailed access and are labelled accordingly. No available-cash balance is invented: sales, collections and gross profit are different quantities, and expenses/withdrawals are not fully tracked.

## Delete business

The owner control is in Settings. Staff and managers cannot invoke deletion, including by calling the RPC directly. Platform owners/admins have a separate control in Business inspection; viewers cannot use it. Both flows require typing the exact business name and checking the permanent-deletion acknowledgement. Admin deletion also requires a recorded reason. The dialog lists consequences, offers Keep business, and confirms completion.

Migration 021 removes business-scoped inventory, sales, repayments, receipts and receipt-edit history, reports, invitations, notifications, subscriptions and staff access in one database transaction. The login account and other businesses are preserved. Account-owned uploads and previously downloaded files remain separate assets/copies. The current device clears the deleted business's records, photos and remembered offline access. Other offline devices and independently exported copies cannot be remotely recalled by this function.

A minimal deletion audit, used-trial claim and checkout reference tombstones are retained. These records cannot restore the business. Preserving the trial claim prevents deletion/recreation from granting repeated free trials. Already registered checkouts for deleted businesses are acknowledged by the signed webhook handler so late events cannot reactivate deleted data. A recent pending checkout must finish or expire before deletion. Deletion itself does not issue a refund, close a merchant account or delete the Countiva sign-in account.

4. Test deletion only on a deliberately disposable test business. Confirm cancel does nothing, a wrong name cannot submit, staff cannot see or invoke deletion, and admin deletion requires a reason. Never use a real customer business merely to test the control.
